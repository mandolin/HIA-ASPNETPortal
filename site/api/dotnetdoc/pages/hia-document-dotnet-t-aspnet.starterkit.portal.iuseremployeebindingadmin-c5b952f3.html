ASPNET.StarterKit.Portal.IUserEmployeeBindingAdminDb

门户账号与员工绑定的后台写入契约。

Administration write contract for Portal-user to employee bindings.

Localized Fields

remarks

本契约只负责单条绑定和解绑，不负责批量导入、外部 HR 同步或密码验证。成功写入后，页面或服务层必须同步写运营审计，并递增目标用户安全版本。

This contract handles only single-row bind and unbind operations, not bulk import, external HR synchronization, or password validation. After a successful write, the page or service layer must record operations audit and increment the target user's security version.
