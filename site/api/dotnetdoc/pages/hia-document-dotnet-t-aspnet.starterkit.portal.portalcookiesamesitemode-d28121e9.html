PortalCookieSameSiteMode

dotnet-type.NET
ASPNET.StarterKit.Portal.PortalCookieSameSiteMode

门户身份与角色 Cookie 的 SameSite 模式。

SameSite mode for Portal identity and role cookies.

Localized Fields

remarks

Unset 表示不下发 SameSite 属性，用于保持与历史部署和 IE9+ 旧浏览器完全一致的行为；None 只在同时启用 Secure 时才有效，否则会被降级为 Unset。

Unset means the SameSite attribute is not emitted, preserving behavior identical to historical deployments and IE9+ legacy browsers; None is valid only together with Secure and is otherwise degraded to Unset.
