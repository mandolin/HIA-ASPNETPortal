remarks

如果图片地址不符合站内浏览策略，控件会隐藏图片而不是回显原始配置值，降低错误配置泄露路径或外链信息的风险。

If the configured image URL fails the portal browse-url policy, the image is hidden instead of echoing the raw setting value, reducing the chance of leaking paths or external-link details.
