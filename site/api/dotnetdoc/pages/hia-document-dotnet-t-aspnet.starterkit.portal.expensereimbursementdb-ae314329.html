ASPNET.StarterKit.Portal.ExpenseReimbursementDb

费用报销数据访问实现；业务数据落在 PortalBiz_ExpenseReimbursements（与员工资料更正同构，独立于结构配置库）。PortalBizDbContext 由 Unity 以单例形式注入外置连接串。

Expense-reimbursement data-access implementation; business data lives in PortalBiz_ExpenseReimbursements (isomorphic to employee-profile correction, isolated from the structural config store). PortalBizDbContext is injected by Unity as a singleton carrying the external connection string.
