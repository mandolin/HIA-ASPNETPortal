ASPNET.StarterKit.Portal.PortalDocumentPolicy

集中定义文档模块的上传、链接、文件名和编辑回跳安全边界。

Centralizes upload, link, filename, and edit-return safety boundaries for the document module.

Localized Fields

remarks

本策略只处理服务器文件上传与现有手填链接。允许扩展名设置只能收紧内置硬允许集， 不能将脚本、页面、配置或可执行文件重新放行。生成的物理文件名用于避免冲突并帮助运维识别， 不是下载授权凭据，也不替代未来的私有文件服务。

This policy handles server-file uploads and existing manually entered links only. The allowed-extension setting can narrow, but never expand, the built-in hard allowlist; scripts, pages, configuration files, and executables cannot be re-enabled. Generated physical filenames prevent collisions and aid operations; they are not download credentials and do not replace a future private-file service.
