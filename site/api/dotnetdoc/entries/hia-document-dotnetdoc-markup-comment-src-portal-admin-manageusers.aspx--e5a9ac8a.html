The role name binds with **`<%#:`** (with colon) rather than `<%#`: the former HTML-encodes before emitting, which is the XSS baseline for controllable data such as role names — do not regress it to `<%#`. The delete button carries no `CommandArgument`; the role to remove is recovered via the DataList's `DataKeyField="RoleId"` in `OnItemCommand`, i.e. "which row" comes from the DataKey and the markup holds no row identifier.

The role name binds with **`<%#:`** (with colon) rather than `<%#`: the former HTML-encodes before emitting, which is the XSS baseline for controllable data such as role names — do not regress it to `<%#`. The delete button carries no `CommandArgument`; the role to remove is recovered via the DataList's `DataKeyField="RoleId"` in `OnItemCommand`, i.e. "which row" comes from the DataKey and the markup holds no row identifier.
