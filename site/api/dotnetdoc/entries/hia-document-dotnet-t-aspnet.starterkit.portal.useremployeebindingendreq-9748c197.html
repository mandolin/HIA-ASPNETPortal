remarks

该对象只描述解绑动作，不承载权限。调用方必须先确认操作者可维护账号员工绑定，并确保解绑不会误删历史关系记录。

This object only describes an unbinding action and does not carry permission. Callers must first confirm that the operator may maintain user-employee bindings and ensure the action does not delete historical relationship records by mistake.
