<section class="oc-section">
  <p class="oc-eyebrow">Way one</p>
  <h2 class="oc-h2">As a ready-made portal foundation</h2>
  <p class="oc-lead">Deployed, you get an intranet site with sign-in, org and authorisation, workflows and auditing already in place, then switch business modules on as needed (employee profile, electricity application and expense claim sample modules ship with the source).</p>
  <div class="oc-grid">
    <div class="oc-card"><h3 class="oc-card-title">Unified identity</h3><p>Account registration, role and entity permissions, org-subtree data scope.</p></div>
    <div class="oc-card"><h3 class="oc-card-title">Workflow and audit</h3><p>Explicit-transition state machines and traceable operational auditing.</p></div>
    <div class="oc-card"><h3 class="oc-card-title">Modular extension</h3><p>Trusted module packages with boundary checks; new business arrives as modules.</p></div>
  </div>
</section>
