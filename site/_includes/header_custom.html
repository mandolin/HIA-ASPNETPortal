{%- comment -%} Language switcher. The site's URL structure is perfectly regular — Chinese at the root, English under /en/, and the two trees are one-to-one (enforced by the bilingual pairing checks in Build-PortalSite.ps1) — so the counterpart URL is derivable from page.url alone: strip the /en/ prefix (en -> zh) or prepend it (zh -> en). The landing pages map / <-> /en/. Rendered via the theme's unconditional `header_custom.html` hook, so it appears on every page including the sidebar-less landing layout. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign u = page.url | default: '/' -%} {%- if u == '/en/' or u == '/en' -%} {%- assign alt_url = '/' -%} {%- assign alt_label = '中文' -%} {%- elsif u == '/' or u == '' -%} {%- assign alt_url = '/en/' -%} {%- assign alt_label = 'English' -%} {%- elsif u contains '/en/' -%} {%- assign alt_url = u | remove_first: '/en' -%} {%- assign alt_label = '中文' -%} {%- else -%} {%- assign alt_url = '/en' | append: u -%} {%- assign alt_label = 'English' -%} {%- endif -%}
