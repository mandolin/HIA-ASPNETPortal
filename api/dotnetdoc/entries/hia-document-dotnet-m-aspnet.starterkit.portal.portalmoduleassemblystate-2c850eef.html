按部署级开关判定当前台账是否允许被接受：开关关闭时，任何调试态条目都使台账不合格（fail-closed）。

Decides whether the ledger is acceptable under the deployment-level switch: when the switch is off, any debug-mode entry makes the ledger unacceptable (fail-closed).
