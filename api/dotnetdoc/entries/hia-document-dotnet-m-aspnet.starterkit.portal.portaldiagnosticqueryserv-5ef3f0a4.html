查询符合受限条件的诊断日志。

Queries diagnostic logs that satisfy restricted criteria.

Localized Fields

remarks

查询先归一化日期和分页，再按文件名日期筛选日志文件。坏行或坏文件不会阻断整个后台页面，而是写入受净化的 Trace 警告。

The query first normalizes date and paging values, then filters log files by the date embedded in their names. Malformed lines or unreadable files do not block the whole administration page; they emit sanitized Trace warnings instead.

params.query.summary

日期、级别、分类和分页等受限查询条件。

Restricted date, level, category, and paging criteria.

returns.summary

当前页诊断记录及截断状态。

Current-page diagnostics records and truncation state.
