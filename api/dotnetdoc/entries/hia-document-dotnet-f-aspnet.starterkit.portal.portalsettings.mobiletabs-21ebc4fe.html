Mobile-compatibility navigation-tab list; its retention scope and future mobile presentation are decided by later planning. Internal deferred-item registration (four-way split: **external Pending — planning undecided**) — basis: whether to keep or retire the mobile-compatibility navigation depends on a mobile presentation plan that does not exist yet, so no decision can be made in this cycle; registered only, not acted on. Owner: product/planning (mobile presentation decision). Re-entry condition: the mobile presentation plan is formed and states this list's fate.
