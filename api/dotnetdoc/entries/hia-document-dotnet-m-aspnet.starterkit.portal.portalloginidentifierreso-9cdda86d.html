把一次登录输入解析为唯一门户用户。

Resolves one sign-in input to a unique Portal user.

Localized Fields

remarks

解析顺序优先使用新用户资料登录名，其次旧用户表名称，再尝试工号，最后合并邮箱结果。前两个唯一字段出现未命中时允许继续向后查找。

The resolver first checks the new profile login name, then the legacy user name, then employee code, and finally merged email results. Not-found results from the first two unique-field checks may continue to later checks.

Resolution result; it may be uniquely found, ambiguous, not found or non-terminal.
