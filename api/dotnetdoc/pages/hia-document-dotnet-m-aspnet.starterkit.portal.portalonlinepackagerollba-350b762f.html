判定一次还原是否达到「字节级一致、零残留」：调用方传入还原后的实际清单， 本方法逐条比对路径/长度/哈希，并断言没有多余文件。

Decides whether a restore achieved "byte-identical, zero residue": callers supply the post-restore actual manifest, and this method compares path/length/hash per entry and asserts no extra files exist.
